Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Brakeline Tools is a sole trader based in England that makes add-ins for Autodesk Fusion, including FlatPack. This policy explains what personal data our software, our website and our support handle, and why. We are the data controller for the data described here. You can contact us at SUPPORT_EMAIL.

The short version

  • Your designs and exported files never leave your computer.
  • FlatPack has no analytics, tracking or telemetry.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

The FlatPack add-in

FlatPack runs on your computer, inside Autodesk Fusion. It reads your design to create flat patterns, DXF files and a summary CSV, and saves them only to the folder you choose.

FlatPack Pro checks that you have bought it by asking the Autodesk App Store's licensing service. To do this it sends your Autodesk user ID and FlatPack's app ID directly from your computer to Autodesk. We don't receive or store this information. Autodesk handles it under the Autodesk Privacy Statement. The free trial makes no network connections.

Your FlatPack settings, and a note of a confirmed purchase, are stored in a .flatpack folder in your user folder on your computer. Delete that folder to remove them.

Buying FlatPack

FlatPack is sold through the Autodesk App Store, and Autodesk handles payment. Autodesk may share limited order details with us, such as your name, email address, country and what you bought, under its publisher terms. We use these only to provide support and to keep the business records UK tax law requires, which we keep for 6 years.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include, such as screenshots or a summary CSV. We use this only to help you, and we delete it within 2 years of our last contact unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering your request.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don't have access to those logs. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and loads its fonts from this site rather than a third party.

Sharing your data

We don't sell your data or share it for marketing. We only share it where the law requires us to.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email SUPPORT_EMAIL and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how FlatPack handles your data, we'll also mention it in the release notes on the Autodesk App Store.
