FlatPack: every flat pattern, cut-ready and costed

One click exports every sheet metal part in your Fusion design to a clean DXF ready for laser, plasma or waterjet cutting, and writes a summary you can quote from.

  • The whole assembly in one click

    Finds every sheet metal part, including sub-assemblies, counts how many of each you need, and creates any missing flat patterns.

  • DXFs your cutting shop can trust

    Curves become polylines, and bend lines are kept or removed as you choose. Stray bend outlines Fusion can leave behind are stripped out, so every closed shape is a real cut.

  • Problems flagged, not hidden

    Broken flat patterns, components with more than one sheet metal body and unused parts are listed at the end, while everything else still exports.

  • A quote before you send it out

    Flat size, cut length, pierces, bends, mass and cost for every part and the whole job, at your own material and cutting rates.
